# Ju's screen portrait

The 466 × 466 opaque WebP is the same levelled crop that Ray selected in the [phone comparison](../../../../hardware/pet-robot/phone/face-size-check.html). The complete square is available for the display to crop.

The source is the project's 1254 × 1254 [Ju portrait](../../../ui/portraits/claude-ju-anime-v2.png). The phone export used a 595-source-pixel field of view. It rotated the line between the pupils at (577, 528) and (783, 472) to horizontal and placed their midpoint at (243, 201) in the output. The canvas background was `#211713` and the WebP export quality was 0.93. The [portrait provenance record](../../../ui/portraits/README.md) covers the source artwork and its licensing.
