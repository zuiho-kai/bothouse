## Highlights

- **An @ is answered where it was written.** A question or small talk to a teammate ("are you there?", "how is it going?") is answered right in the chat channel and opens nothing. When the teammate judges it a real job, she replies with 【接】 on the first line, and only then does the bridge open a workspace, carry the job (with its text, files and what she wrote after 【接】) over and wake her there; the result comes back to the chat channel. This also applies when the lead herself is given a job; a 【问】 keeps the job waiting until the answer arrives (#1758, #1755).
- **Dispatch rules live in one place.** The duty layer, the persona team line and the lead tools no longer carry their own copies of the dispatch rules (personas no longer say "dispatch only with @"); the team-mode rules are the single source, and the size cap covers all of it (#1755).
- **Repair handoffs need a root cause.** `ws-handoff` now sends back a handoff whose title or scope mentions a fix, bug or issue number unless it has a `## 根因` section with a symptom → why → why line and two pieces of command output (whether develop already has a fix, and the commit that introduced the problem). The handoff script also no longer caps workspaces at 8 and queues the rest: a full pool opens a new workspace (#1754).

## Fixes

- **Workspace handoffs keep the whole job**: the handoff row carries the full dispatch text and its attachments, not only the title (#1748, #1751, thanks @jsk11231).
- **`/stop` stops a CLI member**: a running CLI member turn is cancelled through ACP `session/cancel` and its late reply is dropped (#1759, thanks @jsk11231).
- **Image-only replies over ACP**: a reply that is only an image is delivered without a false "empty answer" error (#1752, thanks @jsk11231).
- **Cooling teammates shown as cooling**: the lead's teammate status shows who is in quota cooldown instead of "free", and reassignment is no longer blocked by a stale board todo (#1756).
- **Tests no longer hit the live gate**: `quota-cooling.test.mjs` clears an inherited `GATE_PORT` (#1750, thanks @jsk11231).
- **Run cards**: chat-channel run cards show the latest 3 steps and a count (#1755).

## Improvements

- **Docs site**: a GitHub Pages site with the architecture diagram, three beginner paths and common sticking points (#1746, #1747).

## 重点

- **@ 在哪说就在哪答。** 对队友的问话和闲聊（「在吗」「进度怎么样」）直接在聊天频道回答，什么也不开。队友判断是正经活，第一行回【接】，桥才开工作区，把这件活（正文、附件和她在【接】后写的话）带过去、在那里叫醒她，做完结果交回聊天频道。主角色自己被派活也一样；回【问】时这件活先等着，回答到了再一起转（#1758、#1755）。
- **派活规矩合成一处。** 职责层、人设里的团队一行和主角色工具不再各带一份派活规矩（人设不再写「派活只用 @」），统一以团队模式规矩为准，字数上限把这些一起算进去（#1755）。
- **修 bug 的交接要写根因。** 交接标题或范围里有「修」「fix」「bug」或 issue 号的，`ws-handoff` 要求有 `## 根因` 节：一行「现象→为什么→再为什么」，加两条命令输出（开发分支上是否已有修复、出问题处的引入提交），缺了就退回。交接脚本也不再限 8 个工作区、多的排队，满了直接新开工作区（#1754）。

## 缺陷修复

- **工作区交接带全活**：交接行带上派单的完整正文和附件，不再只有标题（#1748、#1751，感谢 @jsk11231）。
- **`/stop` 能停 CLI 成员**：正在跑的 CLI 成员一轮通过 ACP `session/cancel` 取消，迟到的回复丢弃（#1759，感谢 @jsk11231）。
- **ACP 只回图片**：只有图片的回复正常送达，不再误报「空回答」（#1752，感谢 @jsk11231）。
- **冷却中的队友显示为冷却**：主角色看到的队友状态里，额度冷却的人不再显示「空着」；改派也不再被任务板上的旧待办挡住（#1756）。
- **测试不打正式 gate**：`quota-cooling.test.mjs` 清掉继承的 `GATE_PORT`（#1750，感谢 @jsk11231）。
- **进度卡**：聊天频道的进度卡显示最近 3 步和步数（#1755）。
