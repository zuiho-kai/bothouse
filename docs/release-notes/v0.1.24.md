Big tasks now go to a workspace by themselves, and the 10-minute watch keeps an eye on them to the end. Also after the turn ends, and also after a restart.

## Workspaces and handoff

- **The lead role hands big tasks to a workspace.** A task that takes more than about 10 minutes, or needs code changes, tests or waiting on commands, is put on the task board and handed to a workspace in the same turn. The default delegation policy no longer blocks this handoff (#413).
- **After the handoff, the lead role stops.** It does not wait, sleep or poll the task board in that turn. The chat channel stays free (#417).
- **Workspaces hand results back with [report].** The last reply of the turn starts with `[report]` and is forwarded to the chat channel. When its last task is done, the workspace runs `bothouse session new --after-turn` and frees itself (#417).

## 10-minute watch

- **A running teammate task stays watched after the lead's turn ends.** This is also true when the card is closed, failed, cancelled or accepted. New progress extends the watch. With no progress, it sends one nudge, stops the task and collects the work done so far (#414).
- **After the bridge restarts, running tasks are watched again.** You do not need to hand out a new task first. At the check time, it reports that the run was cut off by the restart and asks you to hand it out again (#415).

## DSH

- **The lead role is selected right after setup.** After the members are imported, the team's lead is selected by itself, so it can open workspaces at once. A lead you picked yourself is not overwritten (#416).
- **The automatic selection does not say "switched by the user".** A switch you make yourself is still confirmed (#418).
- **The workspace instructions give the full request.** One call to `POST /collab/api/rooms/{id}/workspaces` opens the workspace, the task and the 10-minute watch. If a role calls `/tasks` with a workspace ticket, the error points to the right endpoint (#412).
- **The DSH watch sees real progress.** A member's reply that grows in place, or a moving progress card, counts as progress. A reply with only the opening stance line does not (#422).
- **Handbacks reach the chat channel.** A stance in the chat channel is no longer taken as a workspace handback. The workspace's result is forwarded to the chat channel once, and the lead is woken up (#422).
- **The lead accepts or sends back workspace work on its own line.** In the chat channel, write `【验收】#id` to accept, or `【返工】#id reason` to send it back. Rework reopens the task, claims the workspace again and restarts the watch. Other words from the lead no longer change workspace tasks (#422).

## Web chat

- **The "who is doing what" card header follows the real state.** When a workspace hands back and goes idle, the header no longer stays at "In progress" (#421).

## Test results

- **Self-hosted web chat, one role:** on a pre-release test build, the task board, progress cards, workspace, 10-minute watch and memory all passed.
- **DSH:** on a pre-release test build, setup selects the lead role, and the lead opens a workspace on the first turn.
- **Discord:** workspaces and the 10-minute watch are not tested in this round, because of the permissions on the test server.
- **Feishu is not tested.**
- **#421 and #422 were checked by automated tests only.** They have not run end to end on a release build.

## 工作区和交接

- **主角色把大活交给工作区。** 预计超过 10 分钟、要改代码、跑测试或等命令的活，接活这一轮就记上任务板、交给工作区。默认委派策略不再拦这种交接（#413）。
- **交出去以后，主角色收手。** 这一轮不再等待、sleep 或查任务板，聊天频道不被占着（#417）。
- **工作区用 [report] 交回结果。** 一轮最后一条回复第一行写 `[report]`，就转回聊天频道。手上最后一件交完，工作区跑 `bothouse session new --after-turn` 把自己空出来（#417）。

## 十分钟看门

- **主角色这一轮结束后，在跑的派活照样有人看。** 卡关了、失败、取消或已验收，也一样。有新进展就续期；没进展就催一次、停下，收回已有的半成品（#414）。
- **桥重启后，在跑的派活接着看。** 不用等下一次派活。到了检查时间，会报一句执行被重启打断、请重派（#415）。

## DSH

- **开箱就选好主角色。** 导入成员后自动选定团队的主角色，马上就能开工作区。你自己选过的不会被覆盖（#416）。
- **自动选定不提示「用户切换过」。** 你自己切换时照旧确认（#418）。
- **开工作区的写法给全了。** 调一次 `POST /collab/api/rooms/{id}/workspaces`，工作区、任务和十分钟看门一起开好。拿工作区票据调了 `/tasks`，报错会指向正确的接口（#412）。
- **DSH 看门认得出真进展。** 成员的回复原地变长、自己的进度卡在动，都算进展。只有开头表态那一行不算（#422）。
- **交回能回到聊天频道。** 聊天频道里的表态不再被当成工作区交回。工作区的结果转回聊天频道一次，并叫醒主角色（#422）。
- **主角色单独一行验收或返工。** 在聊天频道写 `【验收】#编号` 就完成，写 `【返工】#编号 原因` 就返工。返工会重开任务、重新认领工作区、重挂看门。主角色的其他话不再改工作区任务（#422）。
