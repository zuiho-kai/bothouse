## Highlights

- **One copy of every tool, living in the repo.** The dispatch scripts, the wiki upkeep programs and the bridge's data files each had a second, locally forked copy on the maintainer's machine. Every local-only feature has been merged back, local extras go through a single `_local.py` hook, every data file now has exactly one owner and one reader per language, the bridge syncs the wizard-installed wiki programs to the current release at startup, and every wiki program takes its location from one place. (#2282, #2283, #2284, #2285, #2288, #2292, #2294, #2296)
- **Leaner codebase.** Dead modules are gone: the companion app (archived under tag `archive/companion`), the role-household local client, the voice relay and voice hub used only by companion, and duplicate patches. Eleven upstream platforms that the setup wizard never offered (LINE, Matrix, Webex, Google Chat, Weibo, Yuanbao, Max, Tuitui, WPS AgentSpace, WPS Xiezuo, DingTalk) are removed, about 25,600 lines; an old config that still lists one starts as an offline placeholder with a "removed from bothouse" notice instead of failing. About 1,170 lines of code unreachable on all three systems are deleted, `engine.go` is split into 25 files by topic, and the 1,546-line event loop becomes one method per event. (#2274, #2276, #2277, #2278, #2279, #2280, #2281, #2287)
- **Nothing is dropped silently any more.** Standing rules over the warning line are sent in full and reported once in the channel; five other size limits now either send everything or say exactly how much was cut; a decision block over its limit is sent and reported, and a reply that cannot reach an archived channel is reported in an open one. (#2271, #2275, #2299)
- **Waiting tasks wake on events.** A task waiting on another task wakes when that one is handed back or closed, with no fixed-time or one-week fallback timers. (#2286, #2290)

## 重点

- **每样工具只留一份，都在仓库里。** 派活脚本、wiki 整理程序和桥的数据文件，以前在维护者本机各有一份分叉。本机独有功能都已并回仓库，本机私货只走一个 `_local.py` 口子；每个数据文件只有一个属主，各语言只留一个读取面；桥启动时把向导装的整理程序同步成本版；各整理程序的 wiki 位置只从一处取。 (#2282, #2283, #2284, #2285, #2288, #2292, #2294, #2296)
- **代码瘦身。** 删掉死模块：companion（存档在 tag `archive/companion`）、角色小家本机端、只给 companion 用的语音中继和语音总台、重复补丁。向导里从来没有的 11 个上游平台（LINE、Matrix、Webex、Google Chat、微博、元宝、Max、推推、WPS 智能体、WPS 协作、钉钉）删掉，约 2.56 万行；老配置还写着它们的，按离线占位平台启动并提示「已从 bothouse 移除」，不再起不来。三个系统都跑不到的代码约 1170 行删掉，`engine.go` 按主题拆成 25 个文件，1546 行的事件循环拆成每种事件一个方法。 (#2274, #2276, #2277, #2278, #2279, #2280, #2281, #2287)
- **不再悄悄丢内容。** 本机守则超提醒线也整份照发并在频道报一次；另外 5 处超限照发或写明截了多少；定案块越线照发并报一次，归档频道发不出的回复在开着的频道报。 (#2271, #2275, #2299)
- **等别的活的任务靠事件叫醒。** 等的那件交回或收口就叫醒，不再挂到点兜底和一周兜底。 (#2286, #2290)
