## Task cards and team board

- **Big cards only in workspaces.** Task cards, the "who is doing what" board and sub-agent tables open only in workspace channels. The chat channel keeps the task board summary and its normal per-turn progress. The "who is doing what" board shows which channel and task each teammate is on, and lists work in other channels (#469).
- **Ownership and execution are tracked apart.** A task keeps its owner while it runs in another workspace, so work can be dispatched across workspaces (#450).
- **Paused items fold away.** Paused entries that are merged, closed or idle fold out of the board and can be restored. Items waiting on you show how long they have waited (#457).

## Restart and workspaces

- **Work resumes after a restart.** Turns interrupted by a bridge restart continue on their own (#461), and the board shows them as busy (#468).
- **Late results are not lost.** A `[report]` or `[ask]` from a parked or closed workspace is forwarded to its last chat channel (#466). Answers keep routing to the current workspace owner (#467).
- **Watch progress counts background work.** Background commands in a workspace count as progress for the 10-minute watch (#459).
