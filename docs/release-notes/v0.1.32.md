## Highlights

- **Work does not get scheduled onto an exhausted provider.** Quota resets reported as `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` are parsed and persisted with a deadline, and cron, timer, watchdog and relay dispatch are held until it passes (#547). The deadline survives a bridge restart instead of being forgotten (#550), and it only starts on a reported limit — 95% used no longer triggers it (#555).
- **The primary role and its work move to an available teammate when the quota runs out.** Handoff reuses the existing team paths: the primary role, the open tasks and the workspace handoffs move together, and scheduling stops targeting the exhausted bot (#553). Handing work to a workspace is refused while that project is still cooling, with the recovery time in the message (#554).
- **Every workspace result reaches the chat channel, and the workspace is freed.** A final reply that closes an open handoff is forwarded to the chat channel even without an explicit report; the chat channel lead is woken with the forwarded content; a handback releases the workspace and resets its session instead of leaving it occupied (#564).
- **Workspaces and quota takeover follow the current lead.** Claim, park, watch, auto-return and the task board all read the lead from `lead.json`, lead-only services are re-applied when it changes, and the quota takeover candidate now comes from the effective team binding instead of manual `/bind` entries (#566).

### Fixes

- **Workspace reports no longer land in another workspace.** Handoff records written before the workspace claim check carry a workspace as their chat target; the state file is upgraded to version 2 and those records point at the chat channel that owns the workspace, so `[report]` and `[ask]` reach the right place (#548, #551).
- **Forwarding a workspace report is now visible in the log.** Every path that drops a reply logs a warning, and a successful forward logs `team exit: forwarded` with workspace, parent and chunk count. A cleared handoff record no longer cuts the fallback chain that picks the last chat channel (#556).
- **A Discord reply is no longer posted twice when its preview cannot be deleted.** When deleting the preview fails (rate limit, unknown message, network), the preview is kept and reused as the final message instead of leaving a second copy (#557).

### Improvements

- **A quieter team board.** The board shows only items in work and items waiting on you; finished items and member lines are gone, bot busy/idle no longer edits it, Discord and Feishu stop re-pinning after an edit, and an empty board is deleted rather than kept (#565).

## 重点

- **额度用完时不再往死掉的机器人派活。** 从 `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` 报错里解析额度重置时间并存下截止点，冷却期内 cron、定时任务、看门和 relay 派发都会被拦住（#547）。截止点重启后仍在，不会一重启就忘（#550）；而且只在真的报错时才冷却，用到 95% 不再触发（#555）。
- **额度用完时主角色和手上的活自动交给空着的队友。** 交接复用现有的团队链路：主角色、未完成的任务和工作区交接一起转过去，调度也不再叫醒额度耗尽的机器人（#553）。冷却期内把活交给工作区会被拒绝，提示里带恢复时间（#554）。
- **工作区每个结果都送到聊天频道，干完就把位置让出来。** 结束一轮的回复只要还挂着未关闭的交接，即使没写 `[report]` 也会转到聊天频道；聊天频道的主角色会被以转来的内容叫醒；交回时释放工作区、重置那里的会话，不再空占位置（#564）。
- **工作区和额度接管都跟着当前主角色走。** 认领、挂起、看门、自动回归和任务板都从 `lead.json` 读主角色，主角色文件一变就重新应用只在主角色上跑的服务；额度接管的候选也改成取实际生效的团队绑定，不再依赖手动 `/bind` 记录（#566）。

### 缺陷修复

- **工作区汇报不会再转进另一个工作区。** 工作区认领校验上线前写下的交接记录把聊天目标记成了工作区；状态文件升到版本 2，这些记录改为指向真正拥有该工作区的聊天频道，`[report]` 和 `[ask]` 都能送到正确的地方（#548、#551）。
- **工作区汇报的转发现在能在日志里查到。** 每个丢弃回复的分支都会打一条警告，转发成功会打 `team exit: forwarded`，带工作区、上级频道和分片数；被清空的交接记录也不再切断「最后用过的聊天频道」这条兜底链（#556）。
- **Discord 预览删不掉时不再重复发一条。** 删除预览失败（限流、消息不存在、网络抖动）时保留预览并直接改写成正式回复，不再留下内容重复的第二条（#557）。

### 改进

- **任务板更安静。** 只显示在做的事和等你拍板的事；今天做完和成员行都去掉了，机器人忙闲不再改动任务板，Discord 和飞书编辑后不再重新置顶，空板直接删掉而不是留着（#565）。
