## Highlights

- **Deploy to DSH from the setup wizard.** Pick DSH in step 1. bothouse detects DSH (including the official Windows desktop app), installs it step by step with live progress, lets you choose roles from persona cards with avatars, then **Open DSH** takes you straight to #general with your roles already in the member list.
- **Persona and avatar library.** 12 ready-made personas. Create, edit, copy, delete (with undo) and upload avatars. Personas come in packs: install a pack (drag a zip or paste a link, preview first), enable, disable, uninstall or export it. Discord deploys set the bot avatar for you; DSH members show their avatars.

## Bug fixes

- **DSH channel page no longer goes blank** when a channel already has messages.
- **#general deep link works**: Open DSH lands on the channels panel, and the login carries over because DSH opens on the same host name.
- **Windows DSH detection** finds the desktop app and falls back to the right bash path.

## Improvements

- **Clear errors in the DSH channel page.** Member failures are explained in plain language with a **Configure model** button, and a member shows an "error" state.
- **Typing and busy indicators** for members.
- **Mobile layout**: channel list and member list open as side drawers on narrow screens.
- **Default avatars** use the original five portraits; other personas get a letter badge.

### Install

- **macOS (Apple Silicon + Intel):** `bothouse-mac.dmg`
- **Windows:** `bothouse-windows.exe`
- **Linux:** `bothouse-linux-x64` (Intel/AMD) / `bothouse-linux-arm64` (ARM) (`chmod +x`, then run)
- **Checksums:** `SHA256SUMS`

The packages are unsigned. macOS: drag **Bothouse.app** into Applications, then **right-click → Open the first time**. Windows: if SmartScreen warns, click **More info → Run anyway**.

## 重点

- **向导一键部署到 DSH。** 第 1 步选 DSH，bothouse 自动检测 DSH（含 Windows 官方桌面版），分步安装并显示进度，从带头像的人设卡片里选角色，点 **打开 DSH** 直达 #general，角色已经在成员列表里。
- **头像库与人设库。** 内置 12 个人设，可新建、编辑、复制、删除（可撤销）、上传头像。人设按包管理：装包（拖 zip 或贴链接，先预览）、启用、停用、卸载、导出。部署 Discord 时自动设 bot 头像，DSH 成员带头像。

## 缺陷修复

- **DSH 频道页有消息时不再白屏。**
- **直达 #general 修好**：打开 DSH 直接进频道面板，并用同一主机名打开，登录状态不丢。
- **Windows 上能检测到 DSH 桌面版**，bash 路径找不到时自动回退。

## 改进

- **DSH 频道页报错说人话。** 成员出错时用中文说明原因，并给出「去配置模型」按钮，成员显示「出错」状态。
- **成员「正在输入」「忙」状态。**
- **手机宽度适配**：频道列表、成员列表改为左右抽屉。
- **默认头像**只用原有五张，其余人设显示首字圆标。

### 安装

- **macOS（苹果芯片与 Intel 通用）：** `bothouse-mac.dmg`
- **Windows：** `bothouse-windows.exe`
- **Linux：** `bothouse-linux-x64`（Intel/AMD）/ `bothouse-linux-arm64`（ARM）（`chmod +x` 后运行）
- **校验：** `SHA256SUMS`

安装包未签名。macOS 将 **Bothouse.app** 拖入 Applications，**首次打开请「右键→打开」**；Windows 若弹出 SmartScreen 提示，点「更多信息→仍要运行」。
